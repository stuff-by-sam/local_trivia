Local Trivia for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Local Trivia collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party code. Nothing you type or play is ever sent to the developer, or to any server on the internet.

Local Trivia (it's called Trivia on your Home Screen) is a party game for people in the same room. One phone hosts a game, and the others join it over the same Wi-Fi network. This page describes everything the app keeps, and everything it shares.

What stays on your device

  • Your nickname, so you don't have to type it again.
  • The last game you joined (its address on your network), so you can rejoin it.
  • A rejoin code for your seat in the current game, kept in the device's Keychain. It never syncs to iCloud Keychain and can't be restored onto another device.
  • The round you're writing, if you host: your questions, answers and scoring rules.
  • Your theme, answer markers and app icon.

These are stored only on your device, and are included in your device's own backups like any other app data. Deleting the app deletes them.

What's shared with the other players

A game only works if the phones in it can talk to each other, so some things go to the other devices in your game, over your local network:

  • When you join a game, your nickname and your answers go to the phone hosting it, which shows your name and score to everyone playing.
  • When you host a game, your phone sends the game's name, its questions, the players' names and the scores to everyone who joins.
  • Players without the app can join by scanning the host's QR code. Their browser loads the game from the host's phone, not from the internet.

All of this travels directly between the devices on your network. The app refuses to connect to anything outside it.

Permissions

Local Network

Trivia uses the local network to find games nearby, join them and host them. Without it, the app can't play.

Camera (optional)

Used only to scan a host's QR code to join their game. The image is read on your device and isn't saved or sent anywhere. You can always type the PIN instead.

Drafting questions with Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, a host can have the app draft questions on a topic. Drafting runs entirely on the device, using Apple's on-device model: the topic and the drafts never leave it.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever handles your information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. The app's source code is public, so you can check all of the above for yourself.
