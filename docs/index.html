TRIVIA_

Party trivia for the room you're in. One phone hosts, and everyone else joins on the same Wi-Fi, with the app or any phone's browser.

Support

How to play

  1. One person hosts. Tap Host a Game, write a few questions (or draft them with Apple Intelligence, or import a CSV), and start hosting. The host plays too.
  2. Everyone else joins. Open Trivia on the same Wi-Fi: the game shows up by itself, so just type the PIN from the host's phone. No app? Scan the host's QR code with any phone's camera to play in the browser.
  3. Answer fast. Right answers score more the quicker they are. Standings follow every question, and a podium ends the game.

Troubleshooting

The game doesn't show up on the join screen

  • Make sure every phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the host.
  • Check that Local Network is on for Trivia: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Some guest and public networks keep devices from seeing each other. If yours does, the host can turn on Personal Hotspot and everyone can join that instead.
  • You can always scan the host's QR code instead.

The QR scanner says the camera is off

Turn on Camera for Trivia in Settings → Apps → Trivia, or type the PIN from the host's phone instead.

Players drop out when the host leaves the app

The hosting phone runs the game, so keep Trivia open on it. A quick switch away is fine, and players reconnect by themselves when the host comes back.

I don't see "Draft with Apple Intelligence"

Drafting needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Right after it's turned on, the model may take a while to download. You can always write questions yourself.

How do I show the game on a TV?

Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and choose your Apple TV or AirPlay TV, or connect the phone with an HDMI adapter. The TV becomes the big screen for the room, showing the join code, the questions and the standings, while the phone keeps its own controls.
